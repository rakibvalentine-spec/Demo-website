Case study · AI Voice Agent

An AI voice agent that never misses a call

A full look at how an AI voice agent answers, qualifies and books for a service business, around the clock.

Before

  • Calls missed after hours and during busy periods
  • Staff interrupted constantly to answer the phone
  • Enquiries written on paper or forgotten

After

  • Every call answered instantly, day or night
  • Appointments booked straight into the calendar
  • Each lead logged in the CRM with a summary

How it works

  1. Answer. The agent picks up in a natural voice and greets the caller.
  2. Understand. It works out what the caller needs and answers common questions.
  3. Capture. Name, number and request are collected and confirmed.
  4. Book. It checks availability and books the slot, then sends a confirmation.
  5. Hand off. Complex or urgent calls go to a human with a full summary.

What we set up

  • Custom voice & script
  • Calendar integration
  • CRM sync
  • SMS / email confirmations
  • Call summaries
  • Staff training
